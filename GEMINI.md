# Product Metrics

Product Metrics is a profit optimisation platform for ecommerce advertising: one app for server-side tracking, profit bidding, product segmentation and campaign planning across Google Ads, Meta and ChatGPT Ads. It sends each order’s profit, new or returning status and lifetime value to the ad platforms, so merchants can bid on POAS, new customers and LTV. Machine learning segments every product into custom labels for Shopping and Performance Max, and the campaign plan sets out the ideal campaign structure for those segments. A dashboard shows profit, customers, stock and returns. Use the `product-metrics` MCP server tools for setup and analysis.

For a setup task, read the skill for the store platform and ad channel in this extension's `plugins/product-metrics/skills/` folder, starting with `plugins/product-metrics/skills/product-metrics/SKILL.md`, which routes to the right one. Docs: https://www.productmetrics.io/docs (Markdown index: https://www.productmetrics.io/docs/llms.txt).
